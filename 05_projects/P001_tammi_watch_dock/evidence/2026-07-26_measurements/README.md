These photographs support visible geometry, measurement context, and keepout
planning for P001. They do not approve requirements, dimensions, CAD, printable
output, or printing. Handwritten dimensions remain `user_measured` PO input and
retain the limitations recorded in `PROJECT.md`.

## Privacy Review

The eight files in this directory were visually reviewed before copying. They
do not show a QR code, serial identifier, or IMEI. The supplied back-label
photographs were deliberately excluded because identifiers or portions of QR
codes remained visible despite masking.

The copied JPEG files use JFIF headers and contain no EXIF block; embedded ICC
color-profile data may remain. Duplicate `- Copy` source files were not stored.
